# Security Hall of Fame

This page lists researchers who have reported a vulnerability to PrepAI's
security team and helped us ship a fix. Entries are added only after the
fix has shipped and the reporter has agreed to be listed.

> No entries yet — be the first.

## How to get listed

Report a vulnerability following [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md), wait for
the fix to ship, and tell us whether you want public credit. We will add
your handle (GitHub, Twitter/X, or website) and a one-line summary of
what you reported.
